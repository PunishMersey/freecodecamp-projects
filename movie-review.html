Batman: Knightfall: Part 1

Batman: Knightfall–Part 1: Knightfall explores the breaking of the Knight—both physically and psychologically. With the help of Batman's other adversaries, Bane wears him down in order to deliver a devastating blow—ultimately destroying him as both an icon and an opponent.
